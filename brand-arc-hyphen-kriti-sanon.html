Hyphen Skincare by Kriti Sanon: India's New Mass-Premium Skincare Brand

Celebrity-led. Science-backed. Mass-premium. Hyphen blends authenticity, product efficacy, and accessible pricing for India's clean-girl era.

By Brand Arc Editors•Dec 7, 2025•4 min read

Hyphen Skincare by Kriti Sanon

Hyphen by Kriti Sanon, launched in July 2023, has quickly emerged as one of India's most talked-about celebrity skincare brands. Kriti Sanon entered the beauty industry not just as a brand ambassador but as a co-founder with equity and active involvement, signaling a shift in celebrity entrepreneurship in Indian skincare. Hyphen perfectly combines celebrity credibility, science-backed formulations, and mass-premium accessibility, capturing the essence of the global clean-girl era trend.

Celebrity-Led Collaboration and Business Background

Hyphen was co-founded by Kriti Sanon in partnership with PEP Technologies, the parent company of mCaffeine, alongside co-founders Vaishali Gupta, Tarun Sharma, Saurabh Singhal, and Vikas Lachhwani. This collaboration brought together Kriti's personal skincare vision and the technical, manufacturing, and operational expertise of seasoned entrepreneurs. With an initial investment of approximately ₹30 crore, Hyphen launched with robust R&D, dermatologically-tested formulations, and a strong supply chain.

The brand's growth trajectory has been impressive. Within its first year, Hyphen reached over 1 million customers across 19,000+ pin codes, and by its second year, it reportedly crossed ₹400 crore in revenue, with a repeat purchase rate of around 60%. These figures underline that Hyphen's success is rooted in product efficacy and consumer trust, not just celebrity appeal.

Filling the Mass-Premium Skincare Gap

Before Hyphen, the Indian skincare market lacked brands offering premium ingredients within an accessible price range of ₹299–₹1,500. Hyphen entered this underserved “mass-premium” space, providing advanced actives like peptides, ceramides, and stabilized vitamin C, all in fragrance-free, irritation-free, and gender-neutral formulations. This approach caters to aspirational consumers seeking science-backed, high-performance skincare at affordable prices.

Clean-Girl Era Aesthetic Meets Modern Brand Identity

Hyphen's visual and brand identity aligns with the clean-girl era, emphasizing simplicity, glowing skin, and minimalism. Packaging features soft pastels and pop colors, rounded modern fonts, and uncluttered layouts—signaling freshness, modernity, and a premium yet approachable feel. This aesthetic resonates strongly with Gen Z and millennial consumers, blending style with efficacy.

A Blueprint for Celebrity Skincare in India

Hyphen is more than a celebrity-backed brand—it is a strategic business venture combining authenticity, product science, and mass-premium positioning. By merging Kriti Sanon's skincare philosophy with professional entrepreneurship, Hyphen delivers modern, effective, and affordable skincare that fits India's evolving consumer tastes and purchasing power.
