Zomato's “Super Weather” Campaign Pays a Playful Tribute to Bengaluru

The film takes a playful twist by casting Dino Morea as the Weather God, orchestrating a divine squad responsible for Bengaluru's unbeatable weather.

By Cultural Fuels Editors•Dec 6, 2025•6 min read

Zomato's “Super Weather” campaign lands as an entertaining, culturally rooted tribute to what Bengaluru's young population loves most about its city—its iconic weather, its humour, and its unmistakable vibe. The campaign rollout began with a clever pre-hype outdoor phase, where billboards across the city teased the question, “Why is Bengaluru's weather so super?”, instantly sparking curiosity and social chatter, amplified further when Anil Kumble shared the hoarding on X.

The reveal film then introduced Dino Morea as the playful Weather God, supported by a divine team featuring Ashish Vidyarthi, Niharika NM, and Jordindian, each adding personality, humour and digital-native appeal to the narrative. As highlighted by Brand Equity, Exchange4Media, afaqs!, Social Samosa and MediaBrief, the film humorously suggests that the city's perfect weather is maintained by these “Weather Gods,” who stay happy thanks to timely “oota” delivered by Zomato.

What makes the campaign memorable is that it doesn't just reference Bengaluru's weather—it transforms it into a comedic, culturally rooted experience that mirrors how people actually speak, joke and bond over the city's climate. By blending nostalgia, influencer culture, hyper-local humour and a smart teaser-to-reveal structure, the campaign becomes more than an ad—it becomes a celebration of what Bengaluru feels like, making Zomato appear not just as a delivery service, but as a brand that deeply understands the soul of the city.
